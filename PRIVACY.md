## Overview

NutriMind is a diet and meal-planning app. To use the core features you must create an account with an email address and complete a profile. The Application does **not** send marketing newsletters. Email is used for login one-time codes (OTP) and important service notices related to your account.

## What personal data we collect

### Information you provide

- **Account:** email address used to sign in
- **Profile:** birth date, sex, height, weight (start / current / target), activity level, smoking status, coffee habits, kosher preferences, preferred cuisines, meal times, office days, food dislikes
- **Usage content you enter:** weekly meal plans (generated and stored for you), meal check-ins, snack or "something else" descriptions and calorie notes, cheat-bank entries, weight logs
- **Support / errors:** crash and error messages the app may send to help fix bugs (we try to strip email addresses and phone numbers from error text)

### Information collected automatically

When you use the Application, technical data may be collected by the app or its service providers, such as:

- device type and operating system
- IP address
- app version
- advertising identifiers and similar device identifiers used for ads (via Google AdMob)
- basic information about how the app is used (for example which screen an error occurred on)

### Information we do not collect

The Application does **not** use your device camera, precise GPS location, contacts list, or photo gallery.

## How we use your data

We use personal data to:

- create and secure your account (email OTP login)
- calculate calorie targets and build personalized weekly menus
- save your plans, check-ins, weight history, and preferences
- estimate snack calories from text you enter (AI-assisted)
- show ads
- diagnose crashes and improve reliability
- comply with law and protect against abuse

We do **not** use your email for marketing newsletters or promotional campaigns.

## Legal bases (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on:

- **Contract / service delivery** — account, profile, menus, logs, and core app features
- **Legitimate interests** — security, fraud prevention, crash diagnostics, and improving core reliability, where not overridden by your rights
- **Consent** — where required for non-essential advertising or similar technologies (including through platform / AdMob consent flows where applicable)
- **Legal obligation** — when the law requires us to keep or disclose information

## Artificial intelligence (AI)

The Application uses **Google Gemini** (Google AI) to help:

- generate personalized weekly meal plans from your profile and preferences
- estimate calories for a snack or meal description you type

Data sent for these features may include relevant profile and preference fields and the text you enter. AI processing is used to provide the feature you requested, not to sell your data. Outputs are stored in your account as part of your plan or as values you choose to save.

This is not intended to produce legal or similarly significant automated decisions about you under Article 22 GDPR. You can contact us if you have questions about AI processing that affects your account.
