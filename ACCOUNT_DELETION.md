# NutriMind — Account and data deletion

This page explains how to delete your NutriMind account and the personal data linked to it.

**App:** NutriMind (Android)  
**Developer:** Yaniv Fried  
**Contact:** [nutrimind.support@gmail.com](mailto:nutrimind.support@gmail.com)

## Option 1 — Delete in the app (fastest)

1. Open NutriMind and sign in.
2. Go to **Profile** → **Settings** (or open Settings).
3. Tap **Delete account**.
4. Confirm. Your account and associated data are removed from our systems.

## Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you cannot open the app, email us:

**To:** [nutrimind.support@gmail.com](mailto:nutrimind.support@gmail.com)  
**Subject:** NutriMind account deletion request

Include:

- The **email address** you used to sign in to NutriMind
- The words **Please delete my NutriMind account and associated data**

We will verify the request and delete the account. We aim to complete deletion within **30 days** (sooner when possible), unless a longer period is required by law.

## What is deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove or irreversibly anonymize data tied to that account, including:

- Login / account record
- Profile (weight targets, preferences, kosher settings, etc.)
- Weekly meal plans
- Meal check-ins, weight logs, and cheat-bank entries
- Related error logs where linked to your user id

## What may remain

- Data we must keep for legal compliance (if any), for only as long as required
- Aggregated information that no longer identifies you
- Data held by independent third parties under their own policies (for example advertising platforms), which you may need to manage in those services’ settings

Uninstalling the app alone does **not** delete your account on our servers. Use one of the options above.
